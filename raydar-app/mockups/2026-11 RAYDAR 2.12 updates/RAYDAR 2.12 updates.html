1 · Travel

Change the postcode from the header, and it changes everywhere

Before a postcode is entered, the header still shows Travel, asking for one.

2 · Check a list

Offer to add the list as soon as it's checked

Today "Add all to the med log" sits at the bottom of the results. Instead, as soon as the list is checked, a prompt right under the box asks "Add these 5 to the med log?" with Add all 5 and Not now. The results still show below. Names go in now; the med log then says how many still need details, with a button that opens each one in turn to fill in dosage, frequency and route.
