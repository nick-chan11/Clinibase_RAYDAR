# Mockups: search, test mode, Clinibase branding (Nov 2026)

Open `RAYDAR mockups.html` in Chrome. It's the same page as the published one:
https://claude.ai/artifact/S8Cre9LKxVpLovyg1LgJtF

- `img/`: screenshots of the real RAYDAR 2.9 with each look applied (light and dark), and the test-mode mockups.
- `themes/`: the colour and font settings behind branding options A, B and C (`base-light.css`, `base-dark.css`,
  `optA.css`, `optB.css`, `optC.css`), laid over the app to make the screenshots. These are the starting point
  for building the chosen look into `src/styles.css`.
- `themes/make-screenshots.mjs`: the script that took the screenshots (paths point at a temporary folder; adjust
  before re-running).

The decisions these are waiting on are listed at the end of the mockups page and in `../../PIPELINE.md`.
